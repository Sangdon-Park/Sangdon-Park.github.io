    Semester Roadmap

    16-Week Learning Path

    Weeks 1–4

    Foundations & Delegation

    Set up tools, understand generative AI, write effective instructions, and determine what can be delegated.

    Weeks 5–8

    Requirements & Design

    Define the team project, develop requirements and architecture, and implement from specifications.

    Weeks 9–13

    Quality & Operations

    Testing, debugging, maintenance, deployment automation, coding agents, security, and ethics.

    Weeks 14–16

    Deployment & Retrospective

    Demonstrate a deployed product and reflect on the project's evidence and decisions.
